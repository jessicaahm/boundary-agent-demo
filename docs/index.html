Proposed Pilot Timeline

Identify one valuable AI use case and deploy it in UAT, secured by Vault and Boundary.

Note: The timeline is for a simple agent and may change depending on the complexity of the agent.

  1. Week 1: Day 13 hours - Format: Face to Face, Output: Identify 1 Use case for POC

    By the end of this session, you will be able to identify your top AI Use Cases with Boundary/Vault with defined success criteria and an initial architecture diagram.

  2. Week 1: Day 2 - 51 hour - Format: Team Meeting, Output: Architecture Diagram for 1 Use Case and Demo

    By the end of this session, you will have chosen 1 AI use case and signed off two things we work out together: its initial architecture diagram, and the success criteria for a Minimum Viable Product you can test in your UAT environment.

    Optional: I may also provide you a short demo in my environment to give you a view of how it may look like.

  3. Week 2: Day 1 - 51 hour - Format: Team Meeting, Output: 1 Agent integrated with Vault/Boundary in your UAT

    By the end of this week, you should have 1 simple AI agent in your environment supported by either Vault/Boundary. We will spend a final hour together to close out the POC and hear your feedback.
